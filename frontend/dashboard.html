Welcome to the Dashboard

A fan-made guide to Fatal Frame II: Crimson Butterfly Remake. Raise your camera and step into the lost village.

The story

Twin sisters Mio and Mayu Amakura wander into a forest while chasing a crimson butterfly. They find Minakami Village, a place that has vanished from the world, still bound to a terrible ritual. Mayu is drawn deeper into the village, and Mio must follow to bring her sister home.

Who you will meet

Mio Amakura

The younger twin and the player character. She is timid, but she will face anything to protect her sister.

Mayu Amakura

The elder twin. Her leg injury from childhood has always made her lean on Mio, and the village now calls to her.

The village spirits

Restless souls still walk the houses and paths. Some only mourn. Others will attack when you come near.

How to play

  • Explore. Search the village by flashlight and read the notes left behind to learn what happened.
  • Raise the Camera Obscura. It is the only tool that can hurt a spirit. Look through the viewfinder to find them.
  • Time your shot. Wait until a ghost is close and the frame glows. A well-timed photo deals the most damage.
  • Upgrade your lens. Spend points from battles to make the camera stronger for the next fight.
